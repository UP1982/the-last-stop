# Chapter 1 — Oliver and the Flower Market

## Goal
A small complete, funny, gently sweet 10–15-minute *first-time* journey. Repeat players can finish faster. Avoid timers even though Oliver mentions markets closing.

## Beat 0: Title
Train silhouettes drift through clouds. UI: Start, Continue if save exists, Quit. Start initializes Oliver chapter.

## Beat 1: Dawn Platform (20–35 sec, skippable)
Wide shot: a blue station lamp, rails, Brass the train. Milo adjusts oversized cap. Oliver struggles to balance a flowerpot under a tiny umbrella.
**Oliver:** “Excuse me! Does this one go to Clover Market?”
Milo blinks, nods.
**Oliver:** “Oh, good. My flowers are beginning to think I made it up.”
Camera shows Oliver climbing aboard. A small whistle; fade into train travel. Skipping produces exactly the same chapter and passenger state.

## Beat 2: Scenic travel (10–20 sec)
Train rides through layered trees, raindrops on windows, soft bells. No driving. Player may advance gently if they want; don't block a quick session.

## Beat 3: Something on the tracks
A large branch lies across the rail. Train brakes softly. Brass gives a question-mark whistle. Milo hops down. Camera shifts to free-moving side view. The blockage is obvious on screen.

## Puzzle layout (rough, world units provisional)
`[TRAIN / BRANCH] ---- [WINCH + EMPTY AXLE] ---- [SMALL HILL / SNAIL] ---- [SHED / HANDLE]`

All key devices are recognizable on a short left-right walk. The shed is visibly unlocked. Design a tiny upward step but no precision challenge.

## Beat 4: Mechanism discovery
Approach old crank: tooltip “It needs a handle.” An intact rope visibly connects crank to branch. Player understands cause before searching. In shed, a removable handle is on an obvious shelf. `E` pick up. One carried object shown attached to Milo. `E` at crank inserts handle. `Hold E` (or repeated E with a generous option) turns it gradually; branch is lifted aside. For motor accessibility, prefer press-to-start automatic crank or hold with no time constraint; do not require button mashing.

## Optional secret
Behind a leaf, a tiny snail waves. Interact once. Milo waves back. No currencies, badges or missable compulsory content.

## Beat 5: Return
Track opens with a clunky sound, train whistles. Player can walk back or press “Return to train” once puzzle cleared. Do not force long backtracking.

## Beat 6: Clover Market (25–40 sec, skippable)
Small flower stalls and warm awnings. Oliver steps down, checks flowerpot, then faces Milo.
**Oliver:** “I was worried they wouldn't bloom in time.”
He notices one flower has opened.
**Oliver:** “I suppose they just needed a little trip.”
Oliver leaves a paper flower on the windowsill. Milo taps it with a paw. End card: “A little further down the line…”

## Save checkpoints
Title start; after Oliver boarded; on travel-to-obstacle transition; handle picked/dropped/installed; crank activated; obstruction cleared; train returned; arrival reward granted. Save version and stable mode state; skipping scenes cannot grant reward twice.

## Visual/audio cues
Soft rain, distant wheel hum, warm amber windows against blue-green forest. The branch is a readable dark silhouette. Winch visibly tied to track obstacle; handle has bright but not flashing contrast. When cleared, brief bloom in the environment plus a comforting whistle.

## Acceptance checks
- A new player can infer the winch/branch relation.
- The handle is not lost irrecoverably when dropped.
- Saving while holding handle and loading restores a valid state.
- Skipping either cutscene does not change passenger/reward correctness.
- At most one keepsake granted, even across reloads.
- Entire scene playable without fast reaction time or precision jumping.
